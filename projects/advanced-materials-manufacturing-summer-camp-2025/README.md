## Overview

A month-long engineering research program (May 27 – June 26, 2025) hosted jointly by New York City College of Technology (CUNY) and NYU Tandon School of Engineering. The program provided practical laboratory experience spanning materials characterization, CAD/CAM, powder/resin/filament additive manufacturing, 3-axis CNC milling, coordinate metrology (CMM), and product development.

## Program Curriculum

| Session Dates | Applied Engineering Focus |
| --- | --- |
| May 27 & 28 | Engineering materials characterization, optical metallography, and specimen mounting |
| May 29 | Cybersecurity and intellectual property in additive manufacturing |
| June 2 to 5 | Hands-on 3-axis CNC vertical milling: setups, pocketing, facing, and profiling |
| June 9 & 10 | Parametric mechanical product development using SolidWorks |
| June 11 & 12 | Advanced additive manufacturing: powder bed fusion (PBF), SLA resin, and composite FDM |
| June 16 & 17 | Technology commercialization, innovation frameworks, and venture pitching |
| June 18 | Bio-compatible materials and medical device development via 3D printing |
| June 23 to 25 | Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T) metrology on Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMM) |
| June 26 | Capstone research presentations and industry expert symposium |

## Experience Highlights

### 01 / Materials Testing & Electron Microscopy

Explored optical microscopy and scanning electron microscopy (SEM) paired with energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDS) to analyze microstructural grain boundaries, fracture surfaces, inclusion densities, and elemental composition maps.
