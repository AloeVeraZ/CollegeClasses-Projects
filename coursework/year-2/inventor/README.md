## Core Competencies Demonstrated

- **Parametric Part Modeling:** Complex sketches with geometric constraints, dimension driving equations, extrude, revolve, sweep, loft, fillet, chamfer, and thread features.
- **Sheet Metal Design:** K-factor bend allowances, relief cuts, flanges, hems, and automated flat pattern generation for CNC laser/brake manufacturing.
- **Kinematic Assemblies:** Defining mate, flush, insert, tangent, and transitional joint constraints to model multi-linkage mechanical movement.
- **Exploded Presentations:** Generating exploded assembly trajectories and bill of materials (BOM) callouts (`.ipn`) for assembly instructions.
- **Engineering Drawings:** Creating associative multi-view drawing sheets (`.idw`/`.dwg`), section cuts, detail zooms, geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T), and automated parts lists.

## Finite Element Analysis (FEA)

The archive includes complete finite element stress analysis reports validating structural integrity under simulated mechanical loads:

| FEA Study | Applied Boundary Conditions | Results & Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structural Bracket | Fixed mounting face, 500 N downward shear force | Von Mises stress distribution, safety factor (SF ≥ 2.5), displacement map |
| Cantilever Plate | Fixed pivot edge, distributed surface pressure | Peak stress localized at transitions, deformation contour plot |

> [!TIP]
> Keep `.iam` assemblies in the same directory structure as their referenced `.ipt` part files to preserve file path associations. PDF drawings allow instant review on any device without CAD software.
