# Project #1 — Machined Plate

**Course:** MECH 1101 — Machine Shop  
**Group:** 2  
**Group members:** Angelo Demetroulakos, Enmanuel Espinal, Radhames, and Amani Walcott

## Project overview

The group began with a blank metal plate and progressively laid out, drilled, finished, and shaped it using manual machine-shop processes. The completed work included holes with different finishing requirements, a 1/4-20 tapped hole, a square cut, dimensioned corner details, and hand-finished surfaces.

## Skills and operations

- Layout dye, scribed lines, and center-punching
- Drill selection and staged hole enlargement
- 1/4-20 internal threading with a tap
- Reaming for a finished hole
- Countersinking and counterboring
- Dimensional sanding and surface finishing
- Filing to scribed layout lines
- Team-based process planning and fabrication

## Fabrication log

### Week 1 — September 25

**Starting condition:** Blank plate.

**Work completed:**

- Applied layout ink and scribed the required lines.
- Center-punched the hole locations.
- Center-punched the group number into the plate.

**Photos:** [Week 1 start and end](../../../media/images/machine-shop-project-1/week-01/)

### Week 2 — October 9

**Work completed:**

- Drilled four pilot holes with a No. 5 drill bit.
- Enlarged those four locations to 1/4 inch.
- Enlarged the center hole using a 1/2-inch drill bit.

**Photos:** [Week 2 start and end](../../../media/images/machine-shop-project-1/week-02/)

### Week 5 — October 30

**Work completed:**

- Tapped the smallest hole to 1/4-20 UNC.
- Finished the middle hole with a reamer.
- Countersunk the left hole.
- Counterbored the right hole.

These operations produced four distinct hole features and demonstrated the differences between threaded, reamed, countersunk, and counterbored holes.

**Photos:** [Week 5 start and end](../../../media/images/machine-shop-project-1/week-05/)

### Week 6 — November 6

**Work completed:**

- Sanded the non-factory-cut side until the part measured 3 inches long.
- Cut a square opening 1/2 inch into the top of the stock.

**Photos:** [Week 6 start and end](../../../media/images/machine-shop-project-1/week-06/)

### Week 7 — November 13

**Work completed:**

- Applied layout ink and scribed 0.0625-inch corner details on the top and upper sides of the block.

**Photos:** [Week 7 start and end](../../../media/images/machine-shop-project-1/week-07/)

### Week 8 — November 20

**Work completed:**

- Filed the inked and scribed corners down to the layout lines.
- Sanded the bottom portion of the part to improve its finish.

**Photos:** [Week 8 start and end](../../../media/images/machine-shop-project-1/week-08/)
