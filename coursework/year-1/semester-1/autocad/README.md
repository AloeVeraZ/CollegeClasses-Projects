# AutoCAD — Semester 1

An introductory technical-drafting archive containing **95 files (7.45 MiB)**: 41 AutoCAD drawings, 28 drawing backups, 23 PDF exports, and a small number of supporting files.

## What is included

- Early drafting and geometry exercises such as circles, triangles, arrays, mounts, and the `Weird House` drawing
- Homework sets covering the course progression
- Quizzes and two midterm drawing sets
- Custom title-block development
- `FINAL.dwg` with a matching PDF export
- `.bak` recovery copies preserved with their related drawings

Use the PDF exports for quick viewing and the DWG files to inspect layers, dimensions, geometry, and drawing construction. Multiple similarly named files represent iterations retained from the original working archive rather than curated duplicates.
